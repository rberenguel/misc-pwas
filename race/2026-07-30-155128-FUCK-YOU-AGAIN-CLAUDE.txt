⏺ Good. Here's what changed and why each part matters:

  - _aggression (0.4–1.0): controls two things — gas threshold angle (π/3 to
  2π/3, so aggressive cars floor it even pointing ~120° off target) and brake
  threshold (only fires at ~0.7π to 0.9π depending on aggression). Aggressive
  cars carry speed into corners; physics does the rest.
  - _steerReact (0.03–0.10): lazy steer wheels overshoot the apex and then
  overcorrect on the exit, which is exactly what creates slide-then-correct
  moments.
  - _noise: an accumulated, decaying random signal applied to steer, scaled by
  aggression. It produces occasional mid-corner wiggles that trigger the slip
  threshold and leave skid marks without making the car go completely haywire.
  - maxSpeed (9–12) and grip (0.035–0.055): slight variation per car so they
  have different characters rather than all being identical.
