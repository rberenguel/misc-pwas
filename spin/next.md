# Next Session Roadmap

## 1. Court Corner Targets
- **Targetable Corner Zones**:
  - Add deep court corners (e.g. sharp deuce and ad corner pockets near the baseline / sideline intersection) as explicit selectable target points.
- **Risk vs Reward Dynamic**:
  - **Higher Execution Risk**: The landing distribution randomness operates over a significantly smaller target box; hitting accuracy threshold is stricter, making mishits (sailing long or pulling wide) more frequent.
  - **Defensive Punishment Reward**: Counted as being +1 effective distance unit further away from the defending player relative to normal baseline cells.
  - **Defensive Cost**: Defenders require additional stamina expenditure or suffer elevated reaching / error penalties to return balls struck cleanly into the corners.

## 2. Post-Game Deck Drafting & Optimization ("Deck Fixing")
- **Post-Game Reward Draft Phase**:
  - After concluding each game (service / return cycle completion), trigger a drafting modal presenting a choice of 3 cards to add to the deck:
    - 2 options from cards already in the deck / card pool (reinforcements).
    - 1 option offering a brand new or upgraded shot (e.g. higher-pace flat drive, acute angled drop, aggressive kick serve).
- **Deck Pruning**:
  - Offer the player the option to drop / trim 1 card from their active deck to keep the draw pile streamlined.
- **Shot Specialization & Synergy Mechanic**:
  - The more copies of a specific shot archetype retained in the deck, the higher the mastery and precision bonus when executing that shot (e.g. progressive accuracy bonus or reduced stamina fatigue for practiced stroke patterns).
