What is RSVP?

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation flashes words one at a time at a fixed point, eliminating the eye movements that account for most of reading time. Instead of scanning across lines, your eye stays still while the text flows past — letting you read faster with less fatigue.

Optimal Recognition Point

Words are aligned so the highlighted pivot letter (in orange) sits at your eye's natural focus. Your brain decodes each word before it disappears, without having to hunt for the centre.

Context Window

Faded words on either side provide a lookahead and lookbehind buffer, reducing the tunnel-vision effect of classic RSVP and keeping you oriented in the sentence.
